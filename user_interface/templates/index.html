👋 Welcome to ESOGÜ IFARLAB

This dashboard lets you remotely monitor and operate IFARLAB's dual-arm cell (a UR10e + a Kawasaki RS005L, sharing an AGV rail and SICK cameras). The hamburger menu at the top gives you 4 tabs:

  • 🏠 Dashboard — start/stop the scenarios (Multi-Robot Inspection, UR10e Inspection, Pick & Place, Human-Robot Collaboration), live camera feeds, and the system log.
  • 📈 Data Analytics — charts and analysis of the collected data.
  • 🚨 Anomaly Detection — where anomaly-detection results are monitored.
  • 🕹️ Free Move — drag the robots to a target pose and run it with Plan/Execute, without writing a scripted scenario.

Before running the robots

The dashboard only manages the software side — the hardware itself needs to be physically powered on and ready:

  • UR10e: press the power button on the teach pendant. Once PolyScope boots, tap the (powered-off) panel at the bottom left and press ON, then START, to power up the robot and release the brakes.
  • Linear axis and sensors: plug in the cable in the cabinet.
  • Once both the UR10e and the linear axis are connected, start the External Control URCap program on the teach pendant (load it and press Play) so the ROS driver can take control.
  • Kawasaki: plug in its cable in the cabinet first, then flip up the switch on its controller. Once the robot is powered, turn the motors on from its teach pendant, then launch KRterm 1.2.8 from the desktop.
  • AGV: turn on its ignition switch, then start rosbridge from the server in the cabinet.

See IFARLAB_Kullanım_Rehberi.docx on the desktop for the full, detailed steps.

Safety

  • The 🛑 STOP ALL button in the top right stops everything, at any time.
  • To try things out without touching the real hardware, leave Use Fake Hardware switched on.
  • A scenario or Free Move always waits for an explicit confirmation before moving the real robot (Confirm Robot Ready / Execute) — make sure no one is within reach of the robots before confirming.
